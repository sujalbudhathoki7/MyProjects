About eKishan Initiative

Breaking Market Monopolies & Delivering Real Value to Citizens

For generations, local agricultural markets in Nepal have been controlled by syndicates and middlemen who buy cheaply from hardworking farmers and sell stale, chemically-treated produce to urban citizens at exorbitant rates.

eKishan changes this ecosystem. By utilizing direct supply logistics and digital tools, we ensure farmers receive the proper fair market value for their harvest while consumers get healthy, pesticide-free, fresh seasonal vegetables, fruits, honey, and cheese delivered straight home without markup extortion.
